Exclusivity

Ian believes the parties are philosophically aligned on becoming long-term strategic partners. The purpose of this section is to understand what exclusivity actually means in practice — and what each party expects.

Please tick any exclusivity principle Steve considers important, and use the notes field to add nuance or corrections.

Steve protection
GMG and GKIM do not pursue Steve-introduced laboratories or marketing groups without Steve's involvement.
Relationship protection
Steve remains commercially involved in any opportunities he originates, for the life of those relationships.
Reciprocal protection
Steve introduces labs and marketing groups to GMG/GKIM; GMG/GKIM introduces opportunities to Steve. Neither side circumvents the other.
Telemedicine exclusivity
Steve does not actively build competing telemedicine relationships where GMG can reasonably perform the role.
Ecosystem preferred partnership
Steve and GMG/GKIM become the preferred partners across the whole ecosystem — labs and marketing groups deal with this partnership rather than fragmented providers.
