About GKIM

Built on a belief:
AI-native businesses are designed, not assembled.

GKIM is the team that does that design, and stays to make it work.

Read our story Speak to the team

We are at the beginning of something significant.

Every ambitious CEO I speak to has already made the same decision: AI will be central to how their business competes. That decision is not the hard part.

The hard part is moving decisively without wasting 18 months on initiatives that produce dashboards but no competitive advantage. Without becoming a technology project manager when you should be running the business.

The companies that pull ahead won't be the ones that deployed the most AI tools. They'll be the ones that designed a better business around AI.

Not a vendor. Not a consultancy. A partner with skin in the outcome. That is what GKIM exists to be.

Ian Morrison
Founder & CEO, GKIM

What we stand for

Our principles

01
Design before you build
We define how the business creates value before a single line of code is written.
02
Outcomes over outputs
We measure success the same way you do: the business performs better and makes more money than the competition.
03
Skin in the outcome
We don't hand over and walk away. We stay to operate, measure, and improve alongside you.
04
Business first, technology second
Every technical decision is made in service of a business goal. Never the other way around.
05
Honest over comfortable
We tell you what your business needs, even when it is not what you asked for.
06
Built to evolve
A business system that stops improving stops competing. We design for continuous evolution from day one.
