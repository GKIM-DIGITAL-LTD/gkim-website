About this Foundry

The Home Care Foundry

The Home Care Foundry is GKIM's mission to bring AI-native operations to the entire homecare industry. We work with operators and key stakeholders to drive transformational change in how care is run, and to turn that transformation into real commercial benefit for the businesses delivering it.

GKIM builds the AI-native operating system for homecare. You bring the licensed, staffed, running business. We take out the coordination cost that's eating your margin — and we're paid from the savings we create, so we only win when you do.
