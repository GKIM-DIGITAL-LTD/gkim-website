Strategic Positioning — GMG + GKIM

WHAT WE ARE
MARKETING.
AND TO WHOM.

GMG is the clinical provider network. GKIM is the operating and technology layer. Together, they allow medical solution providers and marketing partners to route patients into a compliant, longitudinal, precision-primary-care model — where medical solutions are prescribed only when clinically appropriate.

Core Positioning Statement
GMG + GKIM is a clinical decision and longitudinal care ecosystem — not a lead-generation company, and not a testing approval engine.

The Two-Layer Model

Clinical Layer
GMG
The clinical provider network. Independent physician evaluation, medical necessity assessment, prescription and referral where appropriate, and ongoing longitudinal patient management. The trusted clinical home into which patients are directed.
Operating + Technology Layer
GKIM
The operating system behind the clinical layer. Workflow design, CRM integration, documentation infrastructure, AI-supported onboarding, consent capture, policy logic, program routing, reporting, and operational coordination. The infrastructure that makes the clinical model scalable.
