Commercial Principles

Fair, transparent, and built to last.

Commercial arrangements follow three principles: fair market value, sustainable economics for all parties, and complete transparency about who does what and who earns what.

Pricing is based on
  • Technology and platform infrastructure
  • Operational support and staffing
  • Workflow complexity
  • Compliance infrastructure
  • AI automation investment
Pricing is never driven by
  • Laboratory reimbursement levels
  • Physician ordering behaviour
  • Medical necessity decisions
  • Clinical outcomes
Topics for the workshop
  • Which services are contracted separately vs bundled
  • Implementation and platform fee structure
  • Launch pricing and scaling principles
  • Periodic fair market value reviews
  • How to onboard additional laboratories
  • How additional marketing groups participate
  • How new healthcare programmes are introduced
