Opportunity Ownership

Clear boundaries. No internal competition.

Ownership is established at the point of origination. Both parties agree not to circumvent the other's exclusive territory, and to collaborate rather than compete for expansion.

Steve's Exclusive Territory
Steve-Originated Opportunities
GKIM will not knowingly pursue these independently while the partnership remains in force.
  • Laboratories introduced by Steve
  • Medical solution providers introduced by Steve
  • Marketing organisations introduced by Steve
  • Strategic opportunities originated by Steve
Steve remains commercial relationship owner during the sales process and continues a relationship management role thereafter.
GKIM's Exclusive Territory
GKIM-Originated Opportunities
Steve may participate where invited, but commercial responsibility rests with GKIM.
  • Opportunities independently generated by GKIM
  • Opportunities introduced by Ian or GKIM staff
  • Existing GKIM relationships
  • Inbound enquiries received directly by GKIM
Customers introduced by Steve become joint customers. No expansion proposal should be made without informing the other party.
Customer Relationship

One integrated team. Four phases.

Customers experience a single, cohesive team — not multiple independent suppliers. Leadership evolves as the relationship matures, but continuity is maintained throughout.

01
Phase One
Opportunity Development
Steve leads
Relationship building, commercial qualification, strategic discussions, and executive sponsorship. Steve drives the conversation; GKIM is introduced as appropriate.
02
Phase Two
Solution Design
Steve — commercial lead GKIM — increasingly present
Joint leadership. Steve continues commercial leadership while GKIM participates more actively in technical and operational discussions.
03
Phase Three
Implementation

GKIM takes the lead on implementation, onboarding, workflow configuration, integrations, and compliance. Steve remains actively involved in executive relationship management.

Customer success becomes shared. Steve manages executive relationships and identifies expansion opportunities. GKIM leads operational excellence, platform evolution, and delivery.
Governance & Terms

Built for continuity.

The partnership is structured to protect customers, honour existing obligations, and ensure service never depends on any single individual.

Quarterly Reviews
  • New opportunities & pipeline health
  • Customer health & satisfaction
  • Operational performance
  • Expansion opportunities
  • Strategic priorities
Exclusivity Commitments
  • Steve makes GKIM/GMG the preferred technology and telemedicine platform for agreed-scope opportunities
  • GKIM does not pursue Steve-originated opportunities independently
  • Both parties agree to avoid circumvention
  • Expansion proposals are agreed jointly before being presented to the customer
Customer Relationship Philosophy
  • The relationship becomes institutional rather than personal over time
  • Customers build direct working relationships with GKIM, GMG, and delivery teams
  • This protects the customer and the partnership from over-dependence on any individual
On Termination
  • Existing customer contracts remain in force
  • Operational services continue uninterrupted
  • Customers retain access to the platform
  • Neither party disrupts ongoing healthcare programmes
  • GKIM assumes full commercial and operational responsibility
A Note on Commission at Termination
Separating new revenue from existing contractual rights.
A fair and durable approach distinguishes between future entitlements and accrued obligations.
After Termination
No entitlement to future commissions on new business or future expansions, unless separately agreed in writing.
Accrued Obligations
Any contractual obligations that arose before termination — fixed introduction fees, defined earn-outs — are honoured in full.
Long-Term Vision

Not built to maximise short-term commissions. Built to create a national AI-native healthcare platform.

Steve as the principal commercial growth partner. GKIM as the technology and operations platform. GMG as the preferred precision medicine clinical infrastructure. And laboratories and medical solution providers gaining a trusted, scalable operating partner they can rely on for the long term.
