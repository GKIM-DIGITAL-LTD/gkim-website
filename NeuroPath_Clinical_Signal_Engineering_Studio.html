Appendix

Core Design Principles

1Signal Before Test

The Objective Clinical Signal is the product. The assay is one delivery mechanism.

2Evidence Before Marketing

Every claim is traceable to supporting evidence. Marketing never outruns validation.

3Judgement Before Automation

AI informs. Clinicians decide. Accountability always remains clinical.

4Reuse Before Reinvention

Every new indication reuses existing knowledge wherever possible.

5Continuous Learning

Every deployment strengthens the platform. Every patient contributes to future understanding.

6Platform Before Product

Products evolve. The Clinical Signal Engineering capability is the enduring strategic asset.
