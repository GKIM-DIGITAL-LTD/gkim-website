Positioning

A Standalone Strategic Initiative

This platform should be considered a standalone strategic initiative, separate from AI Ann. AI Ann may eventually consume and interrogate its outputs, but the platform itself has independent value and should not be viewed as an AI Ann feature.

Strategic Objective

Building a Structured Intelligence Asset

Create a structured intelligence asset that identifies, analyses and prioritises women's health opportunities across key public sector organisations.

  • NHS and Integrated Care Boards (ICBs)
  • Local Authorities
  • Government Departments
  • Public Health Programmes
  • Funding Bodies
  • Commissioning Organisations

The purpose is to transform fragmented public-sector information into actionable commercial intelligence.

Development Sequence

Recommended Build Order

Three phases, executed in sequence. Each phase unlocks the next.

1
Highest Priority
Adora Knowledge Base

Before collecting external intelligence, ensure Adora's own knowledge is organised and accessible. This creates organisational memory, enables the AI Ann foundation, and improves consistency of messaging.

Potential sources:

  • Adora service definitions and women's health expertise
  • Clinical pathways and outcome data
  • Economic impact evidence and white papers
  • Marketing content and founder knowledge
  • Partnership and historical project knowledge

This is likely the highest ROI first step — it provides context for all future intelligence interpretation.

2
Once Internal Knowledge Exists
Automated External Intelligence Collection

Create automated collection pipelines for publicly available information at low marginal cost and large-scale coverage.

Government
  • GOV.UK & Women's Health Strategy
  • ONS and DHSC publications
NHS
  • NHS England and ICB publications
  • Trust and commissioning documents
Local Authorities
  • Public health reports and JSNAs
  • Annual reports and board papers
Procurement
  • Contracts Finder notices
  • Award and procurement notices
3
Precision Instrument — Not Primary Source
Targeted FOI Intelligence Programme

Only deployed after internal knowledge exists and public data collection is operational. Use FOI strategically to answer questions that public data cannot.

  • Service provision gaps and programme utilisation
  • Budget allocation details and regional variation
  • Workforce impact data
  • Procurement intentions
  • Outcome measurement approaches

FOI creates proprietary datasets and differentiated market knowledge unavailable to competitors.
