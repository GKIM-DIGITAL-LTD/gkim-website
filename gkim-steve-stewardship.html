Design Principles

Six principles that govern everything that follows.

The framework is not a set of rules. It is a set of beliefs about how a lasting commercial partnership should work — and why the details fall into place once those beliefs are shared.

01
Opportunity creation has long-term value and must be protected — not gradually eroded as relationships mature.
02
Operational excellence requires GKIM and GMG to retain full control of technology, delivery, compliance, and clinical operations.
03
Customer relationships should become institutional rather than dependent on any individual — protecting the customer, the partner, and the platform.
04
Commercial partners remain strategically involved throughout the customer lifecycle — not just during the sale.
05
Rights are earned through continued contribution rather than granted indefinitely. Stewardship is active, not passive.
06
The model is designed to be repeatable — Steve is the first Commercial Steward in a framework built for a national platform.
Roles & Responsibilities

Each party owns what they are best at. Nothing more.

The model separates commercial opportunity creation from operational delivery. This allows Steve to focus entirely on building relationships, while GKIM and GMG focus on converting those relationships into lasting customer success.

Stewardship Rights

Eight protected rights for every account you originate.

For each customer Steve brings to the ecosystem, the following rights apply. They are designed to ensure Steve's contribution is recognised and protected throughout the life of the customer relationship — not just at the point of introduction.

1
Exclusive Commercialisation Rights
Steve holds the exclusive right to originate and commercialise services into every account he introduces. GKIM and GMG will not knowingly pursue those opportunities independently while the stewardship remains in force.
Protection remains active for the life of the stewardship
2
Executive Sponsor Status
Steve remains the executive sponsor for every stewarded account throughout the relationship — including strategic reviews, commercial planning, and senior customer engagement. Operational relationships within GKIM and GMG may expand, but executive sponsorship stays with Steve.
3
Right of First Refusal on Expansion
When additional commercial opportunities arise within a stewarded account, Steve has the first opportunity to lead the commercial discussion. Only if Steve elects not to pursue the opportunity within an agreed period may GKIM act independently.
  • Additional medical programmes and new disease areas
  • New laboratories or provider groups
  • AI services, billing, and patient operations
  • Technology enhancements and platform extensions
  • New geographies and additional states
4
Participation in Strategic Reviews
Steve is entitled to participate in all scheduled strategic reviews for stewarded customers — covering customer satisfaction, growth opportunities, operational performance, new programmes, and future roadmap.
5
Performance-Based Exclusivity
Stewardship rights continue while Steve remains actively engaged and continues to add value. The objective is to reward ongoing contribution — not passive ownership. Stewardship criteria include:
  • Maintaining executive relationships within stewarded accounts
  • Participating in strategic reviews
  • Supporting commercial development and expansion
  • Maintaining agreed service standards and partnership obligations
6
Team Building Rights
Steve may establish and manage a commercial team to support stewarded accounts — including account managers, business development representatives, customer success personnel, and commercial operations staff. Steve remains responsible for their activities.
7
Assignment & Transfer Consent
Stewardship rights are personal to Steve. They may not be transferred, assigned, or sold without the prior written consent of GKIM — ensuring continuity for customers and protecting the integrity of the ecosystem.
8
Defined Termination Events Only
Stewardship rights terminate only upon clearly defined events. The relationship between GKIM and the customer becoming operationally close is not a termination event — this is an expected outcome of a maturing relationship. Termination events are limited to:
  • Material breach of the partnership agreement
  • Sustained inactivity over an agreed period
  • Insolvency, fraud, or misconduct
  • Repeated failure to fulfil stewardship responsibilities
  • Mutual agreement
Customer Relationship

One integrated team. Four phases. Steve present throughout.

Leadership shifts as the relationship matures — but Steve's involvement never disappears. Executive sponsorship, strategic oversight, and expansion leadership remain with the Commercial Steward at every stage.

01
Phase One
Opportunity Development
Steve leads GKIM — technical support on request
Relationship building, commercial qualification, strategic discussions, and executive sponsorship. Steve drives the engagement. GKIM provides technical support wherever it strengthens the commercial conversation.
02
Phase Two
Solution Design
Steve — commercial lead GKIM — technical solution design
Joint leadership. Steve continues to own commercial discussions and customer relationships. GKIM leads technical and operational solution design. The customer experiences one team.
03
Phase Three
Implementation
GKIM leads delivery Steve — executive sponsor
GKIM owns operational delivery — onboarding, workflow configuration, integrations, and compliance. Steve remains the executive sponsor, maintaining the senior customer relationship and ensuring commercial momentum continues.
04
Phase Four
Long-Term Growth
Steve — strategy & expansion GKIM — operational excellence
Customer success becomes fully shared. Steve identifies strategic expansion opportunities and maintains executive relationships. GKIM leads operational performance and platform evolution. Both parties jointly support long-term customer success.
Governance & Terms

Responsibilities divided. Contribution protected.

The framework is structured so that neither party needs to fear the other. Steve does not fear being replaced as the customer relationship matures. GKIM does not fear losing control of its technology or platform. That clarity makes the partnership durable.

Quarterly Strategic Reviews
  • New opportunities and pipeline health
  • Customer satisfaction and relationship health
  • Operational performance
  • Expansion opportunities across stewarded accounts
  • Strategic priorities for the ecosystem
Exclusivity Commitments
  • Steve makes GKIM and GMG the preferred platform for opportunities within agreed scope
  • GKIM does not pursue Steve-originated opportunities independently
  • Both parties agree to avoid circumvention in all forms
  • Material commercial expansions are agreed jointly before presentation to the customer
Continuity Obligations
  • Existing customer contracts remain in force on any exit event
  • Operational services continue uninterrupted regardless of partnership status
  • Customers retain full access to the platform
  • Neither party disrupts ongoing healthcare programmes
What Steve is Never Responsible For
  • Operational delivery or customer success management
  • Technology, AI platform, or workflow automation
  • Compliance systems or regulatory obligations
  • Clinical governance or physician oversight
  • Implementation or onboarding execution
On Accrued Obligations
Existing contractual rights are always honoured.
Any obligations that arose before termination — fixed introduction fees, defined earn-outs, accrued commission — are honoured in full, regardless of how or why the stewardship ends.
On Future Revenue After Termination
No entitlement to commissions on new business.
After termination, Steve has no entitlement to future commissions on new business or future expansions, unless separately agreed in writing. The value of this model lies in continuing collaboration — not indefinite passive income.
Guiding Principle
"The objective is not to divide ownership. The objective is to divide responsibilities — while protecting the long-term contribution of each party."
Long-Term Vision

Steve as the first Commercial Steward in a framework built for a national platform.

Future commercial partners may each become Stewards for their own territories, sectors, or customer portfolios — while GKIM provides the common AI-native operating platform and GMG provides clinical infrastructure where required. The ecosystem scales nationally without requiring GKIM to own every commercial relationship, and without requiring Steve to own any part of the platform. Each party holds what they built.
