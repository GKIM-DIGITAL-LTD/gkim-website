01 Where Adora is right now
adora

A strong product. A critical moment.

Everything has changed in five years — the market, the regulation, the competition. Adora has moved faster than anyone. Now the priority is building the commercial engine to match.

What Adora has built
  • Class 1 regulated medical device — the only one of its kind in UK menopause
  • Three products: Meno A (digital), Meno B (nurse-led), Meno C (employer bespoke)
  • Real clinical evidence — NHS CivTech pilot, 4,000+ users, B2B NPS in the low 80s
  • Live deal with Legal & General across 2.1m insured lives (~£840K–£1.5M/yr)
  • Lime Health and Medicash integrations in progress
  • A product women genuinely love and trust
What the business needs now
  • A sales and go-to-market system that isn't dependent on Ann alone
  • Cookie-cutter packaging across Meno A / B / C for each buyer type
  • Marketing messages that speak each buyer's language — insurers, EAPs, OH, employers
  • Outcome reporting and ROI evidence built into the product — not bolted on
  • Systems and tools so a small team can punch above its weight
  • A CTO-level partner who can help Ed and the tech team move faster
⏱
The window is 18 months. Adora has a real head start — regulated, proven, live with major partners. The risk is not the product. The risk is being outpaced commercially while the clinical foundation is strong.

02 How GKIM & Ian can help
adora

Three things. One partnership.

Ian Morrison brings fractional CIO capability — strategic, technical and commercial — powered by AI. This is about building systems and tools that work without Ann in every conversation.

a) Supporting Ann personally
  • An AI-powered personal assistant — meeting notes, pipeline tracker, follow-up drafts, all surfaced automatically
  • A decision-support brain trained on Adora's own knowledge, strategy and documents
  • Less time in the weeds. More time on the things only Ann can do
  • A thinking partner who has read everything, knows the strategy, and can be challenged at any time
  • Help preparing board and investor materials — fast
The goal
Ann should never get ill from overwork again. Systems should carry the load.
b) Accelerating the whole business
  • Build the Adora knowledge graph — all the clinical evidence, buyer frameworks, value propositions — structured and queryable
  • Systemise sales: ICP maps, objection libraries, ROI calculators, sales scripts per buyer type
  • Enable the two new salespeople with tools, not just training
  • Build reporting and outcome dashboards so buyers see value automatically
  • Help Ed and the tech team prioritise and move faster on product
  • Position Adora for the insurer channel — the largest long-term prize
The goal
A small team that behaves like a team three times its size.
c) Building real deliverables
  • Not strategy decks. Working tools — live sales assets, ROI calculators, buyer-specific pitch packs
  • Marketing content that speaks each buyer's exact language — from insurer CMOs to OH leads to HR directors
  • Product reporting built to prove value at renewal, not just at sale
  • A digital brain that Ann's team can query, update and trust
  • Infrastructure that compounds — every piece of work makes the next one faster
The goal
Every engagement produces something Adora owns and can use at scale.
🤝
Ian is not a consultant who delivers a report and leaves. He embeds alongside Ann as a fractional CIO — building, thinking and doing — with AI as the force multiplier that makes a small team's effort go very far.
