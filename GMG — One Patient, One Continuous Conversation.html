The medical group

A telehealth medical group that delivers Medicare Annual Wellness Visits (AWV) and then orders whatever the visit calls for — lab tests and further medical services such as CGM, CCM, or pharmacogenomic (PGX) testing — through a network of licensed virtual physicians, while keeping each patient's existing in-person primary care physician informed at every clinical milestone.

GMG isn't a call center or a clinic the patient drives to — it's a licensed medical group whose physicians turn a single phone call into a complete, documented, longitudinal care episode.

The communication strategy

A deliberate CRM — not a mailing list

Every message is tied to a real system event, sent on the right channel, by the right hand. Thirteen message types carry the entire relationship.

1

Every outreach has an objective

We reach out for defined reasons — enroll, confirm, reduce no-shows, activate the care plan, support sample collection, deliver results, and coordinate the PCP. Nothing is random.

2

Multi-channel by default

SMS for immediacy, email for detail and attachments, and fax for clinical offices — so nothing depends on a single inbox. An AI Voice Agent is on the roadmap, to care for patients and get them on the line with a doctor whenever they need.

3

A deliberate playbook for each

11 messages fire automatically; 2 are sent by hand. Cadence is intentional — layered 24-hour then 1-hour reminders to reduce no-shows, and a human walkthrough at the high-friction collection step.

4

The care team, not just the patient

Two messages are addressed to the patient's in-person PCP — so longitudinal care stays connected across both doctors, automatically.

PHASE 2Stage · Consent → Connect → Consultation

The Virtual Visit

The patient gives verbal opt-in consent, is transferred to a care coordinator for clinical intake, then connected to a licensed GMG physician who conducts the Annual Wellness Visit live. This is the heart of the relationship — and notably, the CRM goes quiet.

The visit, step by step
Verbal opt-in consent Clinical intake & profile Connect to the GMG physician Conduct the Annual Wellness Visit
What the physician covers in that encounter
Medications & history A personalized prevention plan Available tests/services — and why Medical necessity & consent The patient's own doctor, kept informed
🤫

Silence is a design choice. No automated message fires during the live encounter — the conversation is happening in real time, with a real clinician. The CRM resumes the instant the visit produces orders and signed documents.

No patient is dropped: if no physician is free at first contact, the patient is warm-transferred via the ring group — or re-booked with layered reminders, with an AI Voice Agent (roadmap) to reconnect them on demand. Consent declines auto-cancel the case and add the patient to the DNC registry.

PHASE 3Stage · AWV Consultation & Ordering

A personalised care plan
